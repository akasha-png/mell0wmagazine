Hi!! This is the 0.5 Newsletter, released on March 14. Mellow Magazine #1 will release on March 27.

I had so much fun going out to concerts and discovering new places while also making new friends along the way. I wanted to combine my love for graphic design and art in this magazine. (All the illustrations you see in this magazine are done on the floor of my bedroom at 3AM.) I want to thank pinterest for my sole inspiration lol.

Even though Magazine #1 doesn't have a title, I wanted it to have a theme of "scared" or "afraid". I was scared cold DMing artists, I was scared to go meet them, and I was especially scared talking to them. But what I realized is that, if I am scared to do it, it will pay off immensely.

So the theme of this "pre-release" message, is to step out of your comfort zone. That's the only way you grow. I used to be really uncomfortable sending DMs or asking for help. But now, it's like a second language to me, and it's a valuable life skill. BE SCARED IN LIFE!!!
